Privacy Policy

AnaWlidi is a private publishing tool used by the owner of the Facebook Page "أنا و وليدي" (Ana w Wlidi) and its linked Instagram account to schedule and publish the Page's own posts.

What data the app handles

The app does not collect, store, sell or share personal data of Facebook or Instagram users. It only uses an access token issued to the Page owner, which is kept on the owner's own computer, to publish posts and to read the Page's own statistics (views, reactions, followers).

Data sharing

No data is shared with third parties.

Data deletion

Because the app stores no user data, there is nothing to delete. You can remove the app's access at any time in Facebook: Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations → remove "AnaWlidi". For any request, contact: yahiaslimani30@gmail.com.

Changes

This policy may be updated; the latest version is always on this page.
