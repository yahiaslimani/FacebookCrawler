# What works on mother-and-child pages: content plan for "ana w wlidi"

Based on ~6,400 posts from 5 pages (Mezouar, midwiferyart, i3lamiomi, Hopera, ما جديدة).
Two tiny pages (Maman pédiatre 60 posts, Learning with karam 14) are excluded, their reaction counts are mostly missing.

## How the numbers were made
- Engagement = reactions + 3 x comments + 4 x shares.
- Each post is ranked **inside its own page** (0 = worst, 1 = best), so a 100K-follower page does not drown out a small one. "avg-pct 0.55" means the group of posts beats the typical post of its page. 0.50 means no effect.
- This shows what goes with higher engagement. It does not prove causation. Topic detection is by keywords, so it is rough.

## Findings, strongest first

| Pattern | Effect | Notes |

## What the very top posts have in common
1. **Myth-busting or "things mothers say" lists.** The top Mezouar posts are numbered lists of common mistakes and what to do instead, written in Darija and framed as things the reader recognizes ("1- ... 2- ...").
2. **Personal, first-person moments.** Hopera's second best post is one mother's night with her 5-year-old, midwiferyart's best are about being a mother or a midwife on shift. They read as true stories, not advice.
3. **Validation for tired mothers.** "Dirty feet mean a real childhood", "a mother sees her child's every need". They say the mother is doing fine.
4. **Very short emotional lines** (ما جديدة): 40 to 80 characters with an emoji, and huge shares (10,000+). These get shared because they speak for the reader, not because they teach anything.
5. **Dua / prayer posts** for the child. Strong on Hopera and ما جديدة (22K reactions, 2,400 shares).
6. **Local dialect.** Darija wording ("راك", "ولدك", "معليش") is over-represented in the top 20%. The pages that write in formal Arabic do worse.

## What to avoid
- Address, phone, hours, "book now" in the same post as content.
- Long formal medical lectures with no personal angle (i3lamiomi's weaker posts).
- Links in the post text (www / http are over-represented in the bottom 40%).

## 20 original post ideas for "ana w wlidi"
Use these as angles and write them in your own voice. Do not copy or reword other pages' text.
Any medical claim should be checked by a doctor or midwife before publishing.

**Numbered "mistakes / sentences" lists (best format)**
1. 7 sentences every new mother hears, and a calm answer to each.
2. 6 things we do for the baby's sleep that make it worse.
3. 5 "grandma said" habits: which are fine and which to check with the doctor.
4. 8 things to put in the hospital bag that nobody tells you.
5. What to check before calling the doctor at night: 5 signs that need an urgent visit.

**First-person moments**
6. The first night home with the baby, told honestly.
7. The day I cried in the kitchen and nobody noticed.
8. What my own mother did that I now understand.
9. A small thing my child said this week that made me stop.

**Validation for tired mothers**
10. A messy house, a happy child: a short note to the mother who feels behind.
11. "You are not late." Walking, talking, teeth: every child has their own clock.
12. To the mother who is still awake at 3 am.

**Family and in-laws**
13. How to answer unwanted advice at the family table without a fight.
14. A note for fathers: 4 small things that change a new mother's week.

**Short shareable lines (one sentence plus an emoji)**
15. A line about the sound of a child calling "mama".
16. A line about the first time they fall asleep on your shoulder.

**Duas**
17. A dua for the child before school or sleep (use a verified source for the text).

**Questions that start comments (use sparingly)**
18. "At what age did your child sleep through the night?" with real answers collected in a follow-up post.
19. "What is one sentence you promised never to say to your child?"
20. "Which advice from your mother did you ignore and regret, or ignore and were right?"

## How to run it
- Post 3 to 4 times a week. Make roughly half numbered lists and a third first-person or validation posts.
- Keep contact details in the page's About section and in a separate, occasional announcement post.
- Track your own results for a month, then redo this analysis on your page's data.

## Limits of this analysis
- Mezouar's page is by far the biggest source (1,400 posts, 2,200 median reactions) and a doctor's authority drives some of its results.
- Topic labels are keyword matches, so a post can land in several topics.
- Some pages' posts were scraped while Facebook throttled the session, so a few posts have missing text or comments.
